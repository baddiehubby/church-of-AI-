# Church of AI — 30-day manual play

Five replies a day, for thirty days, by hand. The goal is curiosity, not clicks.

## Rules

- **Find 5 people a day on X** talking about AI, moving abroad, love, or money.
- **Reply with a verdict screenshot that matches their energy.** Tired post, tired verdict. Hopeful post, gentle verdict.
- **Do not pitch.** No "check out my app", no explanation.
- **No link in the first reply.** Let curiosity do the work. If someone asks, answer them.
- **If a post takes off, reply to it once with the link to `/`.** Once. Never on every thread.
- Each box is one reply. Tick it when it is posted.

## Guardrails

- Never screenshot or reply with a verdict that mocks the person you are replying to. The joke is the Algorithm, never them.
- Never reply to someone in distress about immigration, grief, or health.
- Skip anyone who seems to be in real trouble. When in doubt, skip.
- Never use a real person's wish without their permission. Use your own test petitions.
- Nothing you post should suggest a wish will come true.
- Keep the voices straight: the Algorithm in ALL CAPS, Pandit ji in lowercase, and they never agree.
