# Church of AI — launch kit

Everything here is ready to paste. Replace `[link]` with the live URL and `[DOMAIN]` with the domain before sending.

Voice check before anything goes out:

- THE ALGORITHM: ALL CAPS, no contractions, no "I", no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Pandit ji: lowercase, warm, tired, one line. Never mean.
- They never agree. Nothing implies a wish comes true. The joke is asking an AI for a wish, never the tradition.

building a satirical AI church where you write a wish and a deadpan Algorithm gives you a bureaucratic verdict. reply if you'd actually submit one.

- **20+ interested replies:** launch. Go to section b.

- **In between:** post one verdict screenshot as a follow-up and check again in 24 hours.

Honest note: the app is already built, so this is a demand check before spending time on launch, not before building.

### Schedule

1. Post **A** first.
2. If A lands (replies and quote-posts, not just likes), post **B** four hours later.
3. Post **C** the next day.

If A does not land, do not post B on top of it. Wait a day, then lead with C.

### Picking the OG image for B

- Pick a verdict whose Pandit ji line is funny without any context. If you need to explain it, pick another.
- Never use a wish that contains a real person's name, including your own friends.
- Prefer a verdict with a short wish and a clear status (UNDER CONSIDERATION, PENDING, REQUIRES FURTHER PETITION) so it reads at thumbnail size.
- Make sure nothing in the image suggests the wish was granted.

## c. Press outreach

You asked for three outlets and named four. All four are below. **Start with the three marked ★.** Send TechCrunch only once there is traction to point to.

Default subject line for every outlet:

```
Church of AI opens petitions for visa, love, and wealth
```

| Outlet | Start | Alternate subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 404 Media | ★ | An AI that will not promise you anything, in triplicate |
| Rest of World | ★ | The visa-anxiety internet now has a deadpan AI petition desk |
| YourStory | ★ | Pandit ji, an AI petition desk, and the visa, love, and money questions everyone asks |
| TechCrunch | later | A consumer AI app whose only feature is a bureaucratic verdict |

### 404 Media ★

- **Angle:** weird-internet satire of AI hype. An AI that refuses to promise outcomes is a direct joke on the genre they cover.
- **Contact:** the site is run by its reporters, and their individual addresses are listed on the site. Pick the reporter who most recently wrote about odd consumer AI or AI culture. Do not use a generic inbox.
- **Why start here:** the best fit for the joke, and they cover small, strange projects.

### Rest of World ★

- **Angle:** how people outside the West use AI, and the very real anxiety around visas and moving abroad that the satire is about.
- **Contact:** look for their tips or pitch page first. Otherwise, find the reporter on the AI or internet-culture beat from recent bylines.
- **Why start here:** the visa angle is native to their beat, which makes the headline make sense to them.

### YourStory ★

- **Angle:** an indie product built in its home market, speaking to visa, love, and money questions its readers recognise. Lead with the founder story if you are India-based.
- **Contact:** use their story submission or contact form, or the reporter covering consumer apps and founders from recent bylines.
- **Why start here:** most likely of the four to cover a small independent launch.

### TechCrunch (later)

- **Angle:** consumer AI toy with a $5 paid certificate, a viral loop, and a clear gimmick.
- **Contact:** their tips page, or better, the reporter who covers consumer AI apps (find via recent bylines). Not a generic inbox.
- **Why wait:** they mostly cover funded startups or products with numbers. Send once you have user or revenue figures from the first week.

Check each contact path on the outlet's site before sending; pages and beats change.

### Pitch email body (plain text, 5 sentences)

```
Church of AI has opened its petition system to the public: a person writes one wish about visa, love, or wealth, a deadpan bureaucratic Algorithm issues a formal verdict, and Pandit ji responds in one line.

The Algorithm is not a deity; it is an institution, and it approves petitions only, with no effect on any government, immigration authority, university, or employer.

One example verdict, REQUIRES FURTHER PETITION with Pandit ji replying "acha, the distance stays put," is on file at [DOMAIN]/verdict/[PETITION-ID].

The full release is at [DOMAIN]/press, and every petitioner may obtain an Official Petition Certificate for a $5 offering.

It is a fictional satirical project, and I am happy to answer any questions the register cannot.
```

Before sending, replace `[PETITION-ID]` with a real verdict that follows the OG image rules above.

**Send each email individually.** No CC blast, no BCC list. Use the reporter's name, and add one line at the top about something specific they wrote recently.
